# Ajaia Q4 AI-Native Strategy & Engineering Roadmap

Welcome to the **Ajaia Q4 Strategy** document. This document was imported from a local `.md` file to demonstrate native document ingestion.

## Executive Vision
We empower modern engineering and product teams to move with **extreme velocity** without sacrificing reliability or craftsmanship.

## Key Strategic Pillars
1. **Zero-Latency Authoring:** Sub-millisecond local typing response.
2. **Deterministic Permissions:** Explicit ownership and granular collaborator roles (Editor & Viewer).
3. **Round-Trip Portability:** Ingest raw text and markdown, and export to Markdown, HTML, or PDF.

## Engineering Tenets
- Prioritize depth and correctness over shallow breadth.
- Relational integrity with atomic transactions.
- Human judgment guiding AI-accelerated workflows.

> "A focused, well-reasoned partial solution is better than an overextended build."
